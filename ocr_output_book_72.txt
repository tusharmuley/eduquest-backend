📘 Project Documentation: Expertise Flow Connect
Overview
This is a multi-role platform for expertise verification, resume upload & parsing, AI-powered profile analysis, and company-expert matching. The system supports 3 types of users:
- Admin
- Expert
- Company
User Roles & Access
1. Sign Up / Sign In Flow
Experts and Companies can sign up and log in.
Admins can only log in, and their accounts are manually created by the system owner.
Supabase Auth is used for authentication.
The user's role (admin, expert, or company) is stored in the user_role field in the Profile table.
2. Expert Dashboard
Purpose: Experts upload resumes and build profiles to get matched with company projects.
Expert dashboard data :

Matching Score

Resume upload Storage (Supabase)
System Flow
[Expert] Upload Resume
   ↓
[Supabase Storage] Save in resumes/{user_id}/resume.pdf
   ↓
[Edge Function] parse-resume → extract skills, bio, exp, etc.
   ↓
[Supabase DB] Save in expert_profiles
   ↓
[Admin Dashboard] Shows unverified profiles
   ↓
[Admin] Marks expert as verified ✅
   ↓
[Company] Can now view this expert
